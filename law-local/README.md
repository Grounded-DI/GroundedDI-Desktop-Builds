# LAW Local — Curated Public-Safe Visual Archive

This folder is a concise public portfolio edition of the LAW Local visual archive.

It contains eight selected visuals across the BriefWise workspace, evidence/audit controls, and VerdictBridge. The screenshots are synthetic, QA-rendered, fixture, or previously public-safe artifacts. They show product surfaces and visible workflow states; they are not live production captures.

## Included

- `LAW-Local-Public-Safe-Visual-Archive-Curated.pdf` — the curated visual book.
- `image-manifest.csv` — image-level provenance, captions, safety status, and limitations.
- `AUDIT.md` — scope, exclusions, and publication checks.
- `screenshots/` — the exact image assets used by the PDF.

## Public-safety boundary

No private client information, real case records, credentials, tokens, internal URLs, raw source documents, confidential documents, or identifying metadata are included.

This archive does not prove legal correctness, production readiness, live provider execution, filing readiness, or real-world outcomes. Visible labels such as “Pass,” “Held,” or “Review Required” are shown as fixture/UI states only.

DepoBot is excluded from this curated edition because only one usable public-safe screen was found. The larger local archive remains separate and is not part of this upload.
