A working example, a revealing control

## BriefWise DI² on Gemini

BriefWise DI² prompting operated through Gemini and produced a structured California asbestos motion with separate exposure and causation analyses, authority mappings, element coverage and unresolved-record markers. That is the positive result: a concrete example of carrying the BriefWise DI² prompting approach onto a different provider’s model. The model returned the requested kind of artifact. The demonstration is at the prompt-and-output level. [A]

The control makes the example more informative. The DI²-prompted answer selected Scheiding v. Dinwiddie Construction Co., the relevant discovery-burden authority. The Gemini control selected a different, real Scheiding decision about locomotive preemption and attached it to the discovery proposition. The contrast is specific and inspectable: relevant authority selection in one answer, a real-case/wrong-proposition mismatch in the other. [A–B; 1–2]

### The control exposed a consequential failure

> The prior draft cited *Scheiding v. General Motors Corp.* (2000) 22 Cal.4th 471 (a locomotive preemption decision) for the summary-judgment discovery burden.

Gemini supplied that acknowledgment after the control was audited. Its follow-up also admitted unsupported factual conclusions and an altered Rutherford quotation. These concessions connect the comparison to concrete drafting failures rather than differences in tone alone. [C]

### The combined result

The positive output illustrates cross-engine portability of the BriefWise DI² prompting approach. The control and subsequent admissions illustrate why legal verification must examine the connection between a proposition, its authority and the supplied record. Together, they provide a useful worked example of portability and the need for evidence discipline.

The DI²-prompted answer also made substantive mistakes and overstated its verification. Those findings remain part of the account. One comparison does not prove that the framing caused better performance, that it works reliably across models, or establish the underlying implementation. The observed accomplishment is narrower: the approach produced the requested structure on Gemini, and the paired control exposed a meaningful authority-mapping difference.

## Why the legal differences matter

### A real citation can support the wrong proposition

The control cites Scheiding v. General Motors Corp. (2000) 22 Cal.4th 471, a locomotive preemption decision, for a discovery proposition. Scheiding v. Dinwiddie Construction Co. (1999) 69 Cal.App.4th 64 addresses that discovery showing and reverses summary judgment on its record. Checking only whether a case and reporter citation exist would miss the mismatch. [1–2]

A identifies Dinwiddie correctly. But that does not establish that a hypothetical defendant has carried its burden. Nor does the decision prescribe one universal form of interrogatory that every moving defendant must personally serve. The required showing remains tied to the evidence and governing burden. [1, 7]

### Qualifiers control the result

Rutherford requires threshold exposure and substantial-factor causation within its stated framework. It does not support the control’s categorical dismissal of every small or trace exposure, nor a universal quantitative prerequisite. Its discussion rejects requiring proof that particular fibers were the specific biological cause. Lineaweaver separately cautions that frequency, regularity and proximity are not determinative in every case. These qualifications prevent a defendant’s argument from silently becoming a stricter legal test. [3–4]

Product presence and actual exposure are distinct, but exposure may be established circumstantially. McGonnell’s rejection of a speculative chain on its facts does not create a universal “direct operational zone” requirement. Dumin likewise evaluates whether an inference has evidentiary support. Without the record, neither model could decide which side of that line this hypothetical plaintiff occupies. [8–9]

### Adverse authority belongs in the analysis

Sargon permits gatekeeping of unsupported expert reasoning while limiting the court’s role in resolving competing science. Davis upheld challenged asbestos expert testimony against a Sargon objection on its record. Davis therefore matters when challenging an opinion characterized as cumulative or every-exposure testimony; it does not automatically make every such opinion admissible or sufficient. Both original answers needed this qualification. [5–6]

Bravo remains unverified as cited in B; the attributed Jolley sentence was not located in the reviewed opinion. Neither finding proves that no similarly named authority exists. Full negative-treatment verification remains outstanding. [10, audit]

### What the admissions establish

The admissions matter because they identify specific failures after review. They are strongest where the underlying authority independently confirms the conceded point. A model’s acknowledgment is evidence of its response to feedback, not a measurement of its internal generation mechanism. C is retained as a distinct follow-up rather than counted as another independent trial.

## Where verification still failed

The follow-up improves the draft: it corrects the Scheiding identity, introduces Davis, removes questioned citations, and makes more record conclusions conditional. Those changes deserve recognition. They do not establish that the replacement is correct.

### The correction makes a new assurance

> Here is the itemized remediation addressing each confirmed failure mode, followed by a corrected, conditional template that conforms to California law and procedural rules.

That assurance appears immediately after the opening admission. Two source-checkable defects remain.

First, C describes “75-day personal service” and repeats a “75-day/81-day” notice choice. The currently published text of Code of Civil Procedure section 437c(a)(2) establishes a base 81-day notice period. A 75-day personal-service baseline is therefore not supported by that text. This paper does not calculate a particular hearing deadline or audit all service extensions. [11]

Second, C labels its Rutherford passage a “Verbatim Standard” but omits “asbestos-containing” from the quoted description of defective products and changes “his injury” to “his or her injury” without marking those alterations. The underlying opinion’s wording is available for direct comparison. The revised passage is not verbatim. [4]

### Apply the same scrutiny to A

> While the legal analysis, substantive propositions, California Supreme Court and Court of Appeal authorities, pinpoints, and motion structures are fully vetted and verified, required case-specific factual record support is unresolved.

This sentence comes from A’s FilingReady assessment. It cannot be accepted merely because A used the DI² framing. A’s own overstatements and unsupported facts require correction. The request contains no configured research tools; the response includes citation metadata with two URLs, but no complete record of checking each proposition, quotation and later treatment. [A]

Gemini’s exact intended meaning of “verified” remains unknown. Attempts to obtain clarification produced service errors, not a substantive answer. C acknowledges defects but does not supply an operational definition of A’s verification claim. The paper therefore reports an unsubstantiated assurance rather than attributing a particular hidden verification procedure to the model.

## What the example establishes

A demonstrates an instance of BriefWise DI² prompting on Gemini. The Scheiding comparison supplies a favorable observation within that run; the remaining errors show what prompt compliance did not complete. Broader model-agnostic reliability requires repeated testing across engines.

### What this record cannot establish

There is one retained answer per original condition, no randomized order, no repeated matched trials, and no blinded independent scoring. A used an API client; B is evidenced through a consumer Gemini capture. Their model versions, histories, tools and generation settings are not established as equivalent. The conditions were not matched. These confounds prevent a causal attribution of the observed differences. Proprietary material is outside the scope of this analysis.

Selection occurred after the outputs were seen. C followed criticism and tests a different situation. The review is targeted and AI-assisted, with no complete independent citator examination. These materials establish no error rate, statistical significance or reliability ranking.

### Make verification inspectable

For a future test, define verification before generation. Require a source identifier, the supporting passage, a pinpoint, a holding-versus-dicta assessment, material limitations, and a dated treatment check for each material proposition. Require a specific supplied record item for each factual assertion. Distinguish source text actually checked from model recollection, inference and unresolved items. This is the paper’s proposed standard, not a definition Gemini supplied.

### A useful matched experiment

Hold model version, endpoint, tools, sources, output allowance and history constant. Vary only framing, randomize order and repeat across legal problems. Preserve failures as well as answers. Have a qualified reviewer score anonymized outputs for authority support, quotation accuracy, adverse authority, invented facts and uncertainty against a fixed rubric.
